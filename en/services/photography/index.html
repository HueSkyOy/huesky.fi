FAQ

Photography FAQ

How many images do I actually need?

A website usually needs 15–30. If the images are also going to social media, brochures and recruitment, a set of 60 will carry you for about a year before the same shots start repeating.

What if my team is nervous about being photographed?

Most people are, and it shows in the first frames. That's why I allow time at the start to settle in. I shoot plenty and select the best – nobody has to pose.

Do I get print-quality files?

Yes. Images are delivered both full-size and optimised for social media. Print usage rights are included in the Company shoot and A year's image bank packages.

How quickly do I get the images?

Edited images within a few working days. If you need a couple immediately, I'll send a quick selection on the shoot day itself.
