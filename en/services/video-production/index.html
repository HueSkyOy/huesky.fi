FAQ

Video production FAQ

What if I don't have a script?

Hardly anyone does, and you don't need one. The script comes out of the brief: I ask what you want to say and who you're saying it to, then write the outline. You see it for comments before the shoot day, so you know exactly what we're filming.

Do I have to appear on camera?

Not necessarily. Plenty of company videos work without a talking head – footage, text and sound carry it. If an interview does fit, I'll help you prepare: you get the questions in advance and nothing has to be memorised.

How long should the video be?

About 1.5–2 minutes for a website, 30–60 seconds for social media. Length follows the channel, and most packages give you the same content in both formats.

What if the weather is bad on the shoot day?

Indoor filming goes ahead as planned. Outdoor filming and drone flights move to an agreed backup day at no extra cost.
