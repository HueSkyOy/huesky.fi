Cafe & Bar Tirkku: a website that shows straight away whether the door is open

Cafe & Bar Tirkku in Isnäs swapped a slow WordPress site for a single-page site that shows straight away whether the place is open and what’s on offer.

HueSky designed, coded and photographed the site. The owner updates the menu and opening hours, and running costs dropped from about €200 a year to the price of the domain.

Starting point

Tirkku is a café, restaurant, bar and village shop in the heart of Isnäs. Its customers usually come to the site for two things: is the door open, and what is the kitchen serving.

The old site was built with WordPress and Elementor. It loaded slowly and cost about €200 a year. A WordPress site also needs theme and plugin updates to stay secure – work that does nothing for the owner of a village restaurant.

Solution

The new site is a single page built around those two customer questions. It was hand-built from scratch, without ready-made themes or plugins.

Opening hours first

Right on top of the front-page photo, the site says whether Tirkku is open right now and when it next closes or opens. Below that, the next two weeks are shown day by day, with exceptions marked separately.

The owner writes exceptions as plain text, for example “21.12.–7.1. suljettu” (closed) or “18.12. pe 17–00” (Fri 5 pm to midnight). The site interprets the lines itself and puts them in the calendar. There are no forms to learn, and the Christmas opening hours are handled with the same message the owner would put on the door.

A menu the owner updates

Dishes, prices and photos come from the Sanity content management system. When a price changes or a new dish is added, the change shows on the site right away, and the layout can’t break during updates. The menu is in Finnish and Swedish, and protein options with prices open up under each dish.

The site knows when the kitchen is on a break

In the winter season Tirkku’s kitchen is sometimes closed even though the café and bar are open. The site then says so at the top of the front page, moves the available dishes to the top of the menu and marks the rest as paused. The notice disappears by itself on the day the kitchen reopens.

Photos from the same place

The site’s photos and aerial shots were taken by HueSky. The site and the imagery were created together, so each photo was taken for the exact spot where it’s used.

The largest element on the page renders in 0.9 seconds on mobile and 0.6 seconds on desktop.

  • Costs: from about €200 a year to the price of the domain. The hosting platform and the content management system both run on free tiers at this site size.
  • No cookies or tracking: the site sets no cookies, so no cookie banner is needed. Fonts are served from the site’s own server, and the map only loads when the visitor opens it.
  • No software to update: theme and plugin updates are gone, and with them most of the security worries.
  • Google reads the page as it is: the menu and opening hours are already in the page’s code, and the opening hours are also marked up in a format Google understands.
  • Reliability: if the content management system is briefly unavailable, the site shows the latest saved information instead of a blank screen.

Technology in brief

  • Site: HTML, CSS and JavaScript without frameworks.
  • Content management: Sanity. The owner updates the menu, opening hours and photos in a browser or on a phone.
  • Hosting: Netlify. HTTPS and publishing are automatic, and changes are checked on a preview address before they go live.
  • Photography: photos and aerial shots by HueSky.
