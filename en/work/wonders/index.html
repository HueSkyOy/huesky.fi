Wonders Boxing Academy: a website that calculates the record itself

Helsinki-based professional boxing stable Wonders Boxing Academy swapped an outdated WordPress site for a bilingual site where each boxer’s record is calculated from the logged fights.

The same number shows everywhere, and nobody types it in by hand. HueSky designed, coded and photographed the site, and keeps the fight results up to date under a maintenance agreement.

Starting point

Wonders is a professional boxing stable whose three boxers train in Helsinki and fight around the world. The site’s most important reader isn’t a fan but a promoter or matchmaker weighing up a fight offer, often from abroad. They check three things on the site: the record, the latest fights and who to call.

The old site was a WordPress site HueSky had built earlier, and it no longer answered these questions reliably. The same record read differently on different pages, the latest fights were missing and some links were broken. The front page weighed almost 14 megabytes, so it opened slowly on a phone.

A wrong record is worse than a missing one, because a matchmaker compares the numbers with BoxRec anyway. That’s why the new site started from the principle that every number is entered in one place only.

Solution

The new site is built around the fight: once a fight is logged, everything else updates from it. The site was hand-built from scratch, without ready-made themes or plugins.

The record is calculated from the fights

Each fight is logged with the opponent, result, method, round and venue. Wins, losses, draws and knockouts are calculated from these automatically. The same number shows on the front-page card, on the boxer’s own page and in the stable’s summary.

The content management system checks the fight details before publishing, so a half-finished or contradictory result can’t reach the site.

A fight history you can check

Each boxer’s page shows their whole professional career fight by fight: date, opponent, the opponent’s record at the time of the fight and their country, result, event and any title. The details are entered based on BoxRec, and the profile links there.

The next fight comes up by itself

Once an upcoming fight is logged, it appears on the front page and the boxer’s page with a poster, a countdown and a ticket link. When the result is logged, the fight moves to the history, the record updates and the poster disappears. Nothing has to be remembered and cleaned up by hand.

In English and Finnish

The site is in English first, because fight offers often come from abroad, and fully in Finnish too. Each language has its own addresses, for example /fighters/tomi-silvennoinen and /fi/nyrkkeilijat/tomi-silvennoinen. Search engines are told which pages are language versions of each other.

Countries are chosen from a list in the content management system, so they show correctly in both languages without separate translation.

Photos from the same source

HueSky has photographed Wonders’ boxers at fights and in training for several years, and almost all the photos on the site come from this archive. The site and the photos come from the same maker, so the photos were chosen and cropped for exactly the spots where they’re used.

Publishing with one button

The content management system has an Update site button. Next to it you can see what has changed since the last publish and whether anything has been left as a draft. HueSky logs the results, but the stable’s manager Karre Anttonen has access to the same view and can publish a change too.

Instagram only on click

A boxer’s Instagram posts load only when the visitor asks for them. The page opens quickly, and Instagram gets no information about the visitor before that. The site sets no cookies and doesn’t track visitors, so no cookie banner is needed.

  • One source of truth for the numbers: the record, knockouts and the stable summary are calculated from the same fights, so they can’t contradict each other.
  • A result is logged once: one entry updates the front page, the boxer’s page and the fight history in both languages.
  • Old links still work: the old site’s addresses redirect to the new pages, so links from elsewhere didn’t break.
  • Boxers can be found by name: each boxer has their own page with its own title, description and share image in both languages.
  • Maintenance is just content: WordPress theme and plugin updates are gone, so the time goes into fight results rather than upkeep.

Technology in brief

  • Site: Astro. The pages are built into ready-made files in advance, so nothing runs on a server for the visitor.
  • Content management: Sanity. Fights, boxers and photos are updated in the browser, and the records are calculated from the fights.
  • Hosting: Netlify. HTTPS is automatic, and the site updates from the button in the content management system.
  • Photography: photos by HueSky.
